# c0lornote

**WELCOME TO A NEW WAY OF INTERACTING WITH YOUR PEOPLE**

c0lornote is a vibrant, color-based social platform where people can share their thoughts through beautifully colored notes. Express yourself with colors that match your mood and connect with your community in a fresh, engaging way.

## Features

- 🎨 **Color Expression**: Choose from 8 vibrant colors to express your mood and personality
- 💬 **Simple Sharing**: Post short, impactful notes (up to 280 characters)
- 👥 **Community Feed**: See what others are sharing in real-time
- 💾 **Persistent Storage**: Your notes are saved locally in your browser
- 📱 **Responsive Design**: Works beautifully on all devices

## Getting Started

Simply open `index.html` in your web browser to start using c0lornote!

Or serve it with any static web server:
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8080` in your browser.

## How to Use

1. Enter your name (optional - defaults to "Anonymous")
2. Write your thoughts in the note field
3. Choose a color that represents your mood
4. Click "Post Note" to share with the community

## Built With

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Local Storage API
